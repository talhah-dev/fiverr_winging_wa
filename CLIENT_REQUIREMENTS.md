## 1. Home Page

### Introduction & Positioning
- **Heading/Tagline**: Take your foiling to the next level (Prone | Wing | Downwind | Wake | eFoil)
- **Badge**: WA Owned & Operated
- **Distributor Info**: WingingWA is the Australian distributor for AFS Foils and the Western Australian dealer for Manta Foil Assist.

### AFS — The Foiling Spirit Since 2009
- High-performance foils, wings, and parawings designed to help riders enjoy foiling with confidence across a range of conditions and disciplines (freeride, carving, downwind, surf foiling).
- Designed and manufactured in Brittany, France, by Foil And Co, combining French craftsmanship with advanced manufacturing techniques.
- **Note**: WingingWA does NOT sell or import AFS Boards. Only front wings, masts, stabilizers, fuselages, wings, and parawings.

### MANTA FOIL ASSIST — Innovating in Foiling Since 2002
- Innovating across kite foiling, wing foiling, eFoils, and Foil Assist since 2002.
- Premium Foil Assist systems for catching more waves, riding in lighter conditions, and traveling further with less effort.
- Designed for surf foiling, wing foiling, downwind, and more, bringing electric power to foiling with performance and simplicity.
