[H2] Battery FAQs

[LI] Yes. The Evo Air batteries are specifically engineered for air travel. Each individual pack is 97 Wh which falls under the standard airline limit of 100 Wh. So you can travel in most airlines with the batteries in your cabin luggage without any special approvals.

[LI] Each travel pack includes an individual Battery Management System (BMS) to protect against short circuits and over-discharge, providing an additional layer of protection.

[LI] The GPS-enabled remote provides a real-time battery stats so you always know your remaining battery.

[LI] The Manta Foils app also offers live battery monitoring and performance insights directly on your smartphone.

[P] You can easily track your battery level using the GPS-enabled remote, which provides real-time stats for your ride. Additionally, the Manta Foils app allows you to monitor remaining charge, battery health, and performance insights directly on your smartphone
                                                                                        .

[P] TakeOff batteries require minimal maintenance to stay safe and last longer. There are no serviceable parts inside. Always store batteries in a cool, dry place. Before charging, let the batteries cool down to room temperature, and always use the supplied charger.

[LI] TakeOff Evo: a single Air battery provides about 15 – 20 minutes, two Air batteries give 35 – 40 minutes, a single Endurance battery offers 45 – 50 minutes, and an Air + Endurance combo delivers 65 – 70 minutes of ride time.

[H2] Charger FAQs

[P] The choice depends on how quickly you need to get back on the water and your battery setup:

[LI] Standard Charger: A high speed charger for active riders, charging a single Air battery in 30 minutes or an Endurance battery in 1 hour and 20 minutes. So you can get back on the water right away.

[LI] Lite Charger: A relatively slower option, charging a single Air battery in 1 hour or an Endurance battery in 2 hours and 40 minutes.

[P] All chargers work in tandem with our improved Battery Management System (BMS) and are equipped with Auto-Balancing features i.e. The battery packs self-balance while charging,
                                                                                which prevents voltage mismatches and ensures the longevity of your cells.

[LI] TakeOff Evo: Simply plug your battery into the main unit and turn on your remote. You'll then see the charge level on the remote display. You can also buy a battery checker that directly shows you that charging level while &amp; after charging.

[H3] 2 - Blade Folding Prop

[P] It opens when you accelerate, and folds when you release the trigger. This allows for a very minimal drag when surfing unpowered.

[H3] 3 - Blade Folding Prop

[P] More thrust for faster acceleration, top speed, and heavier riders.Improved efficiency with reduced cavitation at full power.

[H2] Propeller FAQs

[LI] 2 Blade: Perfect for lightweight riders and beginners. If you want more push, ride heavier or with a smaller board/foil, get the 3-Blade.

[LI] 3 Blade: Provides more thrust and higher efficiency, making it ideal for those seeking maximum power.

[P] The 2-blade folding propeller is light and makes it easy to move and turn. The 3-blade folding propeller gives more power and speed, ideal for heavier riders or those wanting a high-performance foil assist setup.

[P] Yes. The 2-blade folding propeller uses less energy, giving longer rides, while the 3-blade folding propeller provides more thrust and speed but can drain the battery faster. Choosing the right propeller helps balance power, speed, and ride time for your TakeOff foil assist system.
